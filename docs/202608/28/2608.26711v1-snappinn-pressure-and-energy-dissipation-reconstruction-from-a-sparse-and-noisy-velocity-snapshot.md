## 摘要
从实验速度测量中重建压力和湍流量具有挑战性，尤其是在没有时间分辨数据的情况下。此外，低播种密度、有限分辨率和测量噪声等限制严重阻碍了精确流场的重建。我们提出了SnapPINN，一种两阶段物理信息神经网络（PINN），它能够从单次稀疏且有噪声的速度数据快照中成功重建三维速度、其空间梯度、压力场，并估计湍动能耗散。本文基于三维DNS湍流管流数据进行评估，SnapPINN采用正弦激活架构，并依次训练解耦的速度和压力子网络。在第一阶段，速度网络拟合粒子数据，同时强制满足不可压缩性，作为一个物理上一致的平滑算子，对噪声正则化速度梯度。在第二阶段，速度网络被冻结，压力网络通过压力泊松方程和预训练的速度梯度进行训练。我们系统性地绘制了SnapPINN在100个测试案例中的重建性能，以模拟具有挑战性的实验条件，例如添加显著的位置噪声、速度场的线性化以及播种稀疏度低至全分辨率DNS网格的0.07\%。定量上，体积速度重建误差在0.5\%以内，而对梯度敏感的能耗散率误差保持在50\%以下，后验推断的$\mathrm{Re}_τ$误差在4%–24%之间，即使在极度稀疏和噪声条件下也是如此。最后，我们建立了一个实用的可靠性图，显示在没有真实值的情况下，哪些实验条件可能产生可靠的SnapPINN重建结果。

## Abstract
Reconstructing pressure and turbulence quantities from experimental velocity measurements is challenging, especially without time-resolved data. Furthermore, limitations such as low seeding density, finite resolution, and measurement noise severely hinder the reconstruction of accurate flow fields. We introduce SnapPINN, a two-stage physics-informed neural network (PINN) that successfully reconstructs 3D velocity, their spatial gradients, pressure fields and estimates turbulent kinetic energy dissipation from a single snapshot of sparse, noisy velocity data. Evaluated here on 3D DNS turbulent pipe flow data, SnapPINN uses a sine-activated architecture with sequentially trained, decoupled velocity and pressure sub-networks. In stage 1, the velocity network fits particle data while enforcing incompressibility, serving as a physically consistent smoothing operator that regularises velocity gradients against noise. In stage 2, the velocity network is frozen, and the pressure network is trained using the pressure Poisson equation and the pretrained velocity gradients. We systematically map reconstruction performance of SnapPINN across 100 test cases to mimic challenging experimental, such as adding significant position noise, linearization of velocity field and seeding sparsity as low as $0.07\%$ of the fully resolved DNS grid. Quantitatively, bulk velocity was reconstructed within $0.5\%$, while errors remained below $50\%$ for the gradient-sensitive energy dissipation rate and within $4$--$24\%$ for the a~posteriori inferred $\mathrm{Re}_τ$, even under extremely sparse and noisy conditions. Finally, we establish a practical reliability map that shows which experimental conditions are likely to yield reliable SnapPINN reconstructions in the absence of ground truth.
