## 摘要
精确的水下航行器运动模型对于自主执行海洋任务（如基础设施检查和科学采样）是必需的。然而，使用传统的基于物理的方法来表征此类运动具有挑战性。本文提出了一种新颖的数据驱动框架，用于学习随机水下航行器动力学。利用欧拉-庞加莱动力学和李群几何，我们开发了一种随机物理信息神经网络架构，该架构尊重水下航行器的物理和几何约束。我们的方法利用保结构随机积分，并基于矩匹配和有限维匹配来确保几何一致的训练。我们在仿真中以及在港口环境中导航码头桩柱的水下航行器上评估了我们的方法。结果表明，我们的方法学习了准确且鲁棒的动力学模型，从而在具有挑战性的海洋环境中实现了安全基于模型的控制。

## Abstract
Accurate models of underwater vehicle motion are needed for autonomous execution of marine tasks like infrastructure inspection and scientific sampling. However, such motion is challenging to characterize using traditional physics-based methods. This paper presents a novel data-driven framework for learning stochastic underwater vehicle dynamics. Using Euler-Poincaré dynamics and the geometry of Lie groups, we develop a stochastic physics-informed neural network architecture that respects the physical and geometric constraints of underwater vehicles. Our approach leverages structure-preserving stochastic integration and builds upon moment matching and finite dimensional matching to ensure geometrically-consistent training. We evaluate our approach in simulation and on an underwater vehicle navigating dock pylons in a harbor environment. The results demonstrate that our method learns accurate and robust dynamics models, enabling safe model-based control in challenging marine environments.
